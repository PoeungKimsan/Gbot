XAUUSD ENGINE DASHBOARD -- RISK & SIMULATION DISCLOSURE

This dashboard presents a SIMULATED / PAPER TRADING system. Nothing shown here is a live
brokerage account, and no real money is at risk.

1. SIMULATED FILLS. Every order shown here was resolved by the engine's own pessimistic
   fill simulator, not by a venue. Limit orders fill only where price traded through the
   level by at least one tick ($0.01). Fills are then charged additional adverse slippage
   and a spread. Where one bar touches both a resting entry and its stop-loss, the engine
   assumes the entry filled and was stopped out inside that same bar. It never assumes the
   favourable sequencing.

2. NOT ADVICE. Nothing here is investment advice, a recommendation, a solicitation, or an
   offer to buy or sell any instrument. This is a research instrument for evaluating one
   rule-based strategy against one market.

3. HYPOTHETICAL PERFORMANCE. The realized P&L, drawdown, expectancy and confidence
   intervals on this page are backtested or simulated results. Hypothetical performance
   has inherent limitations: it involves no financial risk, it benefits from hindsight in
   the simulator's own assumptions, and no representation is made that any account will
   achieve results similar to those shown. Simulated results are not a record of past
   trading in an account.

4. CONFIDENCE INTERVALS ARE CONDITIONAL. A bootstrap interval around the expectancy in R
   is reported only when the sample contains at least 30 closed trades. Below that
   threshold this panel reads "INSUFFICIENT DATA (<30 TRADES)" and shows no bounds, because
   an interval computed from a handful of trades is fabricated precision rather than
   caution. Below the threshold the point estimates are still shown, and they are as
   unreliable as the count implies.

5. FIGURES ARE SNAPSHOTS. The API serves the engine's published projections with a cache
   lifetime of ten seconds, and this dashboard calls a payload stale after thirty. A DATA
   STALE banner means the numbers are old, not that the market has stopped, and every
   figure on screen should be re-read before it is acted on.

6. SOME FIELDS ARE NOT YET PUBLISHED. Where this dashboard shows "NOT PUBLISHED BY THIS
   ENGINE BUILD", the engine does not record or publish that value. Those cells are not
   zeros and not empty results; they are gaps, and the specification of each one is in
   docs/PHASE_5X_SPEC.md. A missing stop-loss is not a missing loss.

7. RISK. Gold (XAUUSD) is a volatile, leveraged market. A strategy that is profitable on
   simulated data can lose money immediately on real data, and trading it can cost more
   than the capital committed to it. Automated systems fail silently: a feed that stops, a
   broker that rejects, a weekday holiday that is not in the calendar.
